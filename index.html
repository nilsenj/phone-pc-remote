Use your Android phone as a trackpad, keyboard and remote for your Windows or Ubuntu computer. Pair with a QR code over your local network. You can also control supported smart TVs.

Connect in three steps

  1. Install the Android app and the Windows or Ubuntu companion.
  2. Connect both devices to a local network where they can reach each other; guest Wi-Fi or device isolation may block pairing.
  3. Open the desktop app and scan its pairing QR code with the Android app. Keep pairing codes private.

Compatibility and common questions

Can I use Android as a mouse and keyboard for Windows or Ubuntu?

Yes. Install the Windows 11 companion from Microsoft Store or the amd64 beta for Ubuntu 24.04 and later. The phone and computer must be able to reach each other on the local network. The computer can use Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

Can I control a TV without a computer?

Yes. Open Add TV in the Android app and follow the prompts for a supported LG webOS, Samsung, or Google TV / Android TV device. Available controls vary by model.

What does Pro add?

Pro includes PC screen mirroring, unlimited saved PCs, and phone-as-webcam on supported Ubuntu setups. Webcam support is unavailable in the Windows companion.
